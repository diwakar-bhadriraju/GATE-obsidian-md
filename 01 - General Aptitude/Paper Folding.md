# Non Verbal Reasoning : Paper Folding

Paper Folding is a type of reasoning problem in which a paper is folded one or more times and then cut or punched. After unfolding the paper, we are asked to imagine and identify the final pattern of cuts or holes. These questions test a person’s ability to visualize shapes, symmetry, and spatial relationships without actually folding the paper.
## How to Solve Paper Folding Questions: Step-by-Step Guide
Follow the below steps to solve any kind of paper folding-based questions.
**1. Observation:** Observe the paper carefully, and note the shape of the paper.
**2. Visualization:** In your mind, carefully visualize the fold. Mostly you will find one-fold questions, which are easy to visualize but sometimes you may encounter more than one-fold questions. 
**3. Elimination Method:** Once you visualize the final outcome, try to use the elimination method wrong answer which clearly can't be the answer. Narrow down your choices.
4. Choose the best match that falls exactly the way you visualize.
