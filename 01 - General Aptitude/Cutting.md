# Non Verbal Reasoning : Paper Cutting

Paper cutting is a reasoning concept where a sheet of paper is folded and then cut at certain places. After the paper is unfolded, we must determine the final pattern or shape formed due to the cuts. This type of problem checks spatial understanding, logical thinking, and visualization skills.
## How to solve Paper Cutting Questions
**Step 1:** Understand the Given Sequence: Carefully look at the sequence of diagrams, and note down the way how paper is folded and/or cut. Pay attention to the direction and orientation of the fold and cut.
**Step 2:** Visualize the Folding and Cutting Operations: Mentally visualize the folding and/or cutting pattern. Try to visualize how the paper will look at each step. 
**Step 3:** Determine the Final Shape: After visualization of each step, determine the final shape. Pay good attention to the sides, and corners of the shapes. 
**Step 4:** Compare your visualization of the final shape with the options provided in the question, and select the one that matches your result.
**Step 5:** Verify Your Answer: Cross-verify your answer, do double-check because a minor cut can play a big role in such types of questions.
